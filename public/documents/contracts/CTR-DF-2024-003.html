National dairy supply covering all Lidl UK retail formats including Superstore, Standard, and Metro.

2. PRODUCT SCOPE

Clause 2.1 Product Scope — Dairy

This agreement covers milk, cheese, yoghurt, and dairy-based products nationally. Fresh produce and fruit categories are expressly excluded.

2.2

Organic and standard lines as per Schedule A SKU list (142 active SKUs at contract execution).

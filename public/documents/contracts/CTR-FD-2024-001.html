1. PARTIES AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Parties

This Agreement is between Lidl Great Britain Limited ("Buyer") and FreshDirect UK Ltd, registered in England (Company No. 08472941), whose registered office is at Unit 12, London Gateway Logistics Park, Essex ("Supplier").

1.3 Southern Region

"Southern Region" means the stores listed in Schedule B, comprising eight (8) retail locations in South West and South East England.

2. TERM AND TERRITORY

2.1 Term

The Agreement commences on 1 April 2024 and continues until 31 March 2027 unless terminated earlier in accordance with Clause 9.

2.2 Territory

The Supplier shall supply Products exclusively to the Southern Region stores specified in Schedule B. National or cross-regional supply requires prior written consent from the Head of Procurement.

3. COMMERCIAL TERMS

3.1 Contract Value

Estimated annual contract value: £4,200,000 excluding VAT, based on forecast volume of 2.1 million units per annum across the Southern Region.

3.2 Pricing

Pricing is fixed for the first 12 months per the Price List (Schedule D). Subsequent adjustments limited to 3% per annum or CPI (Fresh Produce), whichever is lower.

3.3 Payment

Payment terms: 30 days from date of invoice, subject to receipt of compliant delivery documentation, quality certificates, and temperature logs.

4. DELIVERY AND LOGISTICS

4.1 Delivery Programme

Deliveries shall be made to each store per the Delivery Programme (Schedule C), typically Monday–Thursday for weekday shelf replenishment.

4.2 Temperature Control

The Supplier shall use temperature-controlled vehicles maintaining 2–8°C for all fresh produce throughout transit. Temperature logs must accompany each delivery.

4.4 Distribution Centre

The Supplier's primary distribution centre for Southern Region fulfilment is the London Sorting DC, Thames Gateway.

5. SERVICE LEVELS

Clause 5.1 Service Level Agreement — On-Time Delivery

The Supplier shall achieve an on-time delivery rate of not less than 95% across all scheduled deliveries within the Southern Region, measured over any rolling 14-day period. A delivery is considered delayed if arrival occurs more than 2 calendar days after the agreed delivery date.

5.2 Quality Rejection

Quality Rejection Rate shall not exceed 2% of delivered volume per calendar month.

5.3 Performance Reporting

The Buyer shall measure SLA performance using its supply chain telemetry platform and publish weekly scorecards to the Supplier.

6. QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE

6.1

All Products must comply with BRC Global Standard for Food Safety (Issue 9) and UK Food Safety Act 1990.

6.2

Shelf-life on arrival must be not less than 85% of total labelled shelf-life for each SKU.

7. CONTINGENCY AND BACKUP SUPPLY

7.1

The Supplier acknowledges that uninterrupted supply of fresh produce is critical to store operations and customer satisfaction.

7.2

The Buyer has designated Total Produce Ltd (Contract CTR-TP-2023-008) as the Backup Supplier for Southern Region produce categories.

Clause 7.3 Contingency Supply — Backup Vendor Activation

Where the Primary Supplier's on-time delivery rate falls below 95% or delay events exceed 30% of deliveries within any rolling 14-day period in the Southern Region, the Buyer may activate the Backup Supplier (Total Produce, CTR-TP-2023-008) for up to 35% of contracted Southern region produce volume, for a maximum period of 90 days, subject to Executive Supply Chain approval.

7.4

Upon backup activation, the Primary Supplier shall cooperate with transition logistics and provide delivery history for the preceding 90 days.

8. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

8.1

The Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 per occurrence.

9. TERMINATION

9.2

The Buyer may terminate immediately for material breach including persistent SLA failure (3 consecutive months below 90% on-time).

14. PENALTIES AND REMEDIES

14.1

Service credits apply for quality rejection rates exceeding 2% per month.

Clause 14.2 Service Level Penalty — Logistics Delay

For each delivery cycle where on-time performance falls below the agreed SLA in Clause 5.1, the Supplier shall pay a fixed penalty of GBP 4,200 per affected cycle, recoverable within 30 days of invoice.

14.3

Penalties are without prejudice to the Buyer's right to activate backup supply under Clause 7.3.

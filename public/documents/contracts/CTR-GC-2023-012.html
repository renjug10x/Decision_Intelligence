1. PARTIES

1.1

Agreement between Lidl Great Britain Limited and Greencore Ready Meals Ltd (Company No. 02904587), Northampton, UK.

1.2

National supply agreement covering chilled ready meals across all Lidl UK regions and store formats.

2. PRODUCT SCOPE

Clause 2.1 Product Scope — Chilled Ready Meals

This agreement covers chilled ready meals, prepared salads (chilled), and convenience food categories only. Fresh produce, fruit, and vegetables are expressly excluded from the scope of this contract.

2.2

Product innovation pipeline reviewed quarterly with Category Management. NPD submissions require 90-day lead time.

3. DELIVERY AND DISTRIBUTION

3.1

National distribution from Northampton and Warrington chilled distribution centres. 6-day delivery cycle to all stores.

3.2

Chilled chain maintained 0–4°C. Remote temperature monitoring on all vehicles.

4. SERVICE LEVELS

4.1

On-time delivery target: 94% measured over rolling 14-day periods nationally.

4.2

Fill rate target: 98.5% of ordered lines per delivery cycle.

5. COMMERCIAL

5.1

Annual value approximately £5,600,000. CPI-linked pricing cap 2.5% per annum after year 1.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1

Four-year term from 1 January 2023. 90-day termination notice after initial 24 months.
